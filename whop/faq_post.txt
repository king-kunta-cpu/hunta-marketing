Everything people ask before buying Hunta, in one place.

Does anything send without me?
No. Every application arrives in Discord as a card with the job, match reason and PDFs. Nothing goes out until you tap Approve.

Which jobs does it search?
17 job boards by default (22 supported) across South Africa, Zimbabwe and Zambia. One employer is never contacted twice within 30 days, and daily caps keep volume sane.

Can I try it before paying?
Yes. The free demo pack runs the whole pipeline on 1 candidate, capped at 25 drafts and 5 sends per rolling 30 days: https://gethunta.pages.dev/
