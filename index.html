The stack in one breath

Hunta hunts 17 job boards by default (22 supported) four times a day across South Africa, Zimbabwe and Zambia,
scores every ad against the candidate's real profile, drafts a tailored cover letter
and a country-formatted CV as PDFs, and posts ONE card to your Discord. You tap
approve — it sends from the candidate's own Gmail. You tap reject — the PDFs are
deleted on the spot. Nothing sends without you, nothing is invented, drafts die at
72 hours, and the whole thing hosts for $0 on GitHub Actions. The demo is free
forever: one candidate, no card — 25 drafts + 5 sends per rolling 30 days, the exact
same build the paying tiers run. No fetch waits longer than 45 seconds, no provider
longer than 30, no run longer than 35 minutes — a hunt finishes or fails loudly, with
the log streaming the whole way.

Product, precisely

Guard-rails (say these unprompted)

• Nothing sends without a human tap — there is no auto mode, it does not exist.
• The writer may only reorder/rephrase the profile; inventing is hard-forbidden.
• Four CV designs (classic · indigo · emerald · sunset), chosen PER CANDIDATE — the coach picks
  the look, the letter stays business-plain, every design ATS-parseable.
• 20 sends / candidate / day — the candidate's Gmail reputation is sacred.
• PDFs: deleted on send/reject, purged at 72 h. Untouched drafts expire after 14 days.
• Store: job metadata + status, plus the pending draft — text NULLed and PDFs unlinked the
  moment of approve/reject; settled rows expire at 60 days. Candidate = one YAML = right to
  erasure; tenant = one file = rm.
• No LinkedIn login, no profile scraping — public job search only.
• Gmail = app password over SMTP; no Google Cloud project, no OAuth, no consent screen.
• Hosting default = two GitHub Actions workflows; $0, no card, nothing to sleep.
• Hard time budgets everywhere: 45 s per URL total, 30 s per LLM provider then instant
  failover, 35 min per run — a stalled hunt is a logged failure, never a silent freeze.
• Candidate documents never enter the git state branch: state is metadata-only by code,
  and the runner untracks any stray PDF before every snapshot.
• --dry never marks a job seen: testing cannot eat tomorrow's leads.
• Demo caps are code, not policy — an atomic ledger in the same wheel; at the cap the
  approve button itself pitches the upgrade.

Architecture one-liner (for technical buyers)

hunters (18) → rule filter → zero-cost pre-score → ONE score+draft LLM call →
Jinja2/WeasyPrint PDFs (ZA/ZW/ZM) → SQLite (metadata only) → Discord card →
signed approve/reject → SMTP send from the candidate's mailbox → purge.
The application ledger lives on a private git branch as a single squashed snapshot commit;
candidate documents never enter git.
Secrets Fernet-encrypted at rest. Every network call runs under a wall-clock budget
(45 s URL / 30 s provider / 35 min run) with unbuffered streaming logs. Optional bare
operator dashboard gated by HUNTA_ADMIN_TOKEN; hosted-customer control center on a
Cloudflare Worker + D1 (free tier, opt-in); self-hosted licence via Cloudflare Worker
(read-only on expiry, never bricked).

Market & ICP

Why now

• Job search in ZA/ZW/ZM is still board-by-board, manual, and tab-heavy — the pain is
  weekly and universal.
• Coaches charge per bundle (R600–R1500) but eat hours producing them — margin is time.
• AI made drafting cheap, but trust did not follow: the market rewards the tool that
  keeps a human in the loop and can prove it.
• Hosting collapsed to $0 (GitHub Actions), so a full pipeline can be given away free —
  the demo IS the marketing budget.
• Payments localised: Whop cards/crypto, Lightning via coinos, Mukuru grants — the rails
  the region actually uses.

Rationale (so every seller believes the numbers)

• Demo R0 forever is the marketing budget: ~1 hunt-minute/day on Actions, cents of LLM on
  the customer's own key. Upgrade trigger is natural — the second candidate.
• Solo $306/mo (about R4,999) ≈ 3–5 CV-and-letter bundles at prevailing coach rates (R600–R1500) plus the
  8–12 h of hunt-and-rewrite time it deletes. The memo's month-1 maths: 4 × $306 (about R4,999) ≈ R20k.
• Annual prepay is the close that funds the build: $2,754/yr (about R45,000, 25% off) — one deal ≈ $2.75k,
  cash now beats MRR while the runway is $0. Offer it everywhere; never discount monthly.
• Studio $613 (about R9,999) is priced for throughput: a studio earns it back in one extra order a week;
  white-label PDFs are the wedge (studios sell their brand).
• Sovereign $920 (about R15,000) undercuts a junior recruiter-day and kills the two real agency
  objections: data residency and lock-in. Onboarding day included because self-host
  failures are support tickets.
• Never discount monthly in public; annual is the only lever. Private discounts for
  universities, NGOs, cohorts of 10+.
• Never per-send pricing — it incentivises spam and contradicts the guard-rails story.

FAQ

Does it apply automatically?

No. There is no auto mode. Every send is a human tap — a design guarantee, not a setting.

Will it invent experience?

No. The writer may only reorder/rephrase the profile. Check the sample CV against the sample letter, line by line.

Spam risk?

Candidate's own mailbox, real ads, human-approved, 20/day cap. The opposite of spray-and-pray.

Data?

Store = job metadata + status + the draft awaiting your tap; the tap deletes both the text and the PDFs, and settled rows expire after 60 days. Candidate documents never persist to the git state branch — the runner untracks stray PDFs before every snapshot (enforced since the 2026-09-19 hardening). Candidate file = one YAML = right to erasure.

LinkedIn?

No login, no scraping. Public job search only.

Boards?

22 boards supported, 17 on by default: Adzuna · Google for Jobs (JSearch) · PNet · CareerJunction · JobMail · Bizcommunity · VacancyMail · iHarareJobs · JobsZimbabwe · GoZambiaJobs · Impactpool · LinkedIn · Himalayas · Jobicy · Remotive, plus ReliefWeb (NGO/UN) once its API name is approved. Careers24 and UNjobs are supported but off by default (they block datacentre IPs). CVs: ZA/ZW/ZM formats.

Cost to run?

$0 default — GitHub Actions. Customer brings a free-tier LLM key + a Gmail app password. No cloud console, no card.

Why is the demo free forever?

Because the demo IS the marketing budget and the upgrade trigger is natural: your second candidate. "Free trial" implies a bill; "free forever" implies confidence.

What does upgrading unlock?

More candidates (3/15/unlimited), more sends, white-label PDFs (Studio), self-hosting (Sovereign). The pipeline itself is identical at R0.

What if a board dies?

Hunters fail alone. The run logs "[hunter:x] 0 jobs" and the rest deliver. Coverage degrades gracefully.

Can it run unsupervised without getting stuck?

That's the 2026-09-19 hardening: every URL gets a 45 s total budget, every LLM provider 30 s of politeness before instant failover, every run a 35-minute cap — and logs stream live from the first second. A hung hunt is a logged failure, never a silent freeze.

Is the free demo a crippled app?

No fork, no watermarks — the identical wheel and the same 22 supported boards (17 on by default). The ledger caps output at 25 drafts + 5 sends per rolling 30 days; at the cap, the pipeline itself tells you what upgrading buys. Licence flips the flavour env; nothing else changes.

The pipeline, end to end

One loop, six steps, four times a day. The buyer sees the card; this is what happens underneath — and the exact places where a human is load-bearing.

  1. Hunt — cron 0 4,8,12,16 * * 1-6 UTC (06:00/10:00/14:00/18:00 SAST, Mon–Sat). Each board scraper runs and fails alone: a dead board logs [hunter:x] 0 jobs and never kills the run.
  2. Pre-score — free rule/domain/seniority/country filters before any LLM spend. Zero-cost triage is the margin.
  3. Match & draft — the LLM scores each role against the candidate's truth file and drafts a tailored letter. Groq 429s auto-failover to Gemini.
  4. Render — country-formatted PDFs (cv_za with ID/EE/licence, cv_zw, cv_zm) plus cover letters.
  5. Approve — the card lands on Discord/Telegram with ✅/❌; the human tap is the only trigger for a send.
  6. Send — SMTP from the customer's own mailbox; results persist to the hunta-state branch and rendered PDFs purge after 72 h.

Nothing between draft and send is automatic. That single human gate is what we sell — and what the press copy already says.

Legal & compliance (quick notes, not legal advice)

• POPIA/GDPR posture: metadata-only store, 72 h purge, YAML = erasure, no profile
  scraping, sends from the data subject's own mailbox with their approval. Say this
  plainly; it is a selling point AND the compliance story.
• Candidate consent: the candidate (or their coach with mandate) supplies the YAML and
  approves sends — consent is structural, not a checkbox.
• Employer-facing: applications are truthful (profile-sourced) and human-approved; no
  misrepresentation risk by design.
• Boards: public search only, polite rates, no login. If a board objects, that hunter is
  removed — coverage degrades, legality doesn't.
• Marketing claims: never promise outcomes (interviews/placements). Promise process:
  coverage, drafts, approval, retention.
• The demo is a product tier, not a trial — say "free forever", never "free trial", to
  avoid auto-billing expectations and refund disputes.
